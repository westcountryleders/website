The Fighter I game was a five-player table and, by all accounts, a friendly and relaxed one, right up until the final round. Marc took the win with 10 points, with Anthony one point behind on 9 and Matt on 8. James and Richard both finished on 7, with James placed ahead on initiative.

The Federation of Sol gave the Titans of Ul quite the beating, spamming out infantry with their breakthrough. The Ral Nel Consortium then brought 7 or more ships to the fight, and with Imperial in hand they only just took Mecatol Rex off the Titans. Meanwhile, a sneaky Sardakk N'orr relished all the fighting and clinched the win by attacking the Vuil'raith Cabal in their home system. Good game, all!
