The Cruiser game was the tightest finish of the weekend. Joe took the win with the Yin Brotherhood on 10 points, with four players right behind him on 9: Dominic, Ben (Blobbox), Conrad and Ben (B’hrian), separated by initiative order. Mike completed the table on 8.

Joe and Dominic later talked through the game, including threat perception, table politics and win slaying, on Joe’s *Through the Wormhole* podcast. Mike, playing the Crimson Rebellion, said afterwards that Joe had wrecked his game plan in round 1!
