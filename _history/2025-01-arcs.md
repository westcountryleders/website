On Sunday 26 January 2025, West Country Leders held our very first **Arcs** tournament, at Excelsior Games in Bristol. 18 players came along to battle it out across the Reach, only a few months after the game was released.

### How it worked

To fit everyone in, we ran two 9-player competitions side by side. Everyone played a 3-player game in Round 1, and the winner of each game went through to their competition’s final, so the day ended with two finals and two champions. Players who didn’t make a final stayed on for a second, more casual game.

### The champions

Congratulations to our two champions, **Hugo** (Affectionate Isopod) and **Art** (BioTech), both from the Bristol University board games society! Each took home one of El’s 3D-printed Arcs trophies and £15 of Excelsior Games vouchers. Hugo said his final came down to a single dice roll, where any of the three players could have won it.

A big thank you to everyone who came along to our first Arcs event, to Excelsior Games for hosting us, to Nick and Liam for running the day, and to El for making the trophies.
