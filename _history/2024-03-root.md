Our third Root tournament, and our biggest so far, took place on Saturday 23 March 2024 at Excelsior Games on Bond Street. Tin (The Foil) designed a brilliant poster for us, and with sign-ups promoted on Reddit and beyond, only four places were left the day before.

![The West Country Leders Spring Root Tournament 2024 poster, designed by Tin (The Foil)](/assets/img/history/root-2024-03-poster.jpg)

### How it worked

Everyone played two games, using Advanced Setup with the Exiles and Partisans deck, all ten factions, and a random map (Autumn, Winter, Lake or Mountain). After two games, the highest scorers went through to the final, with points awarded as follows:

- 4 points for a dominance win
- 3 points for a win
- 1.5 points each for the winners of a coalition
- 1 point for coming second (0 for the other players)

House rules included an Advanced Setup redraw if the whole table agreed, Despot Infamy for the Vagabond, and three plot tokens of each type for the 🐦‍⬛ Corvid Conspiracy (12 plots in total). Three-way coalitions were banned! The day ran from 10:30 to the final at 19:00, with time for lunch and dinner in between, and everyone taking part got 10% off in Excelsior.

### Prizes

Leder Games kindly provided store vouchers for the top four: $75 for 1st place, $55 for 2nd, $45 for 3rd and $25 for 4th. Everyone who won a game also went home with a little jar of algae!

### The champion

Congratulations to **Dan**, who achieved greatness with a heroic win in the final, leading the 🐀 Lord of the Hundreds to victory. He even went home with a laminated Champion certificate!

![Dan's laminated Spring Root Tournament Champion certificate, Bristol 2024, signed "Dan (Rats)"](/assets/img/history/root-2024-03-champion-certificate.jpg)

A big thank you to everyone who came: it was our biggest Root tournament so far, and it was lovely to see everyone playing, chatting and hanging out. Thank you to Liss and the team at Excelsior Games for hosting us (and for saying how clean and tidy we left the space!), to Tin (The Foil) for the poster, to Leder Games for the prizes, and to Liam, Nick, James, Rick and Joe for organising the day.
