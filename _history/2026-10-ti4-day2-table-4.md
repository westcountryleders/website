Francesco's Naaz-Rokha Alliance took the Carrier with 10 points, holding all of his home planets and the first initiative slot. It was a fine result in the face of repeated rulings during the game that weakened the Naaz-Rokha's super mech.

Behind him it was extremely tight, with Chris's Xxcha Kingdom, Filip's Barony of Letnev (Filip travelled from Belgium to join us!), Conrad's Mahact Gene-Sorcerers and Gavin's Argent Flight all finishing on 8 points. Initiative order separated them, with Chris in second, then Filip, Conrad and Gavin. Horst's Arborec finished on 7 points, but with a heroic effort, building a war sun as the Arborec.

| Place | Player | Faction | Points | Home planets | Initiative |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | Francesco (winner) | Naaz-Rokha Alliance | 10 | All held | 1 |
| 2 | Chris | Xxcha Kingdom | 8 | All held | 3 |
| 3 | Filip | Barony of Letnev | 8 | All held | 4 |
| 4 | Conrad | Mahact Gene-Sorcerers | 8 | All held | 6 |
