The leaders of the Cqaark were no match for their competitors, with Rich’s Yssaril Tribes taking over the Galactic Council. Matt’s Council Keleres managed to maintain their sway on Mecatol Rex, albeit now subservient to the Guild of Spies. Miriam’s Barony of Letnev, battered and bruised, had to bow even lower than the Keleres, despite taking their home world and having unlimited fleet capacity in the final round.

This Galactic Council were also big fans of War Suns: every time one was built, a giant round of applause was given to the dock workers who made it happen. However, the real winner was Brian, the Destroyer of Worlds, otherwise known as the cute yellow rubber duckie.
