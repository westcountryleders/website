Our October 2026 Twilight Imperium tournament, *Leders of the Imperium*, took place at Bristol Independent Gaming, starting on Saturday 10 October with a huge turnout of 54 players across 9 tables of 6 on Day 1.

![Day 1 at Bristol Independent Gaming, with each of the nine tables numbered](/assets/img/history/ti4-2026-10-day1-everyone-tables.jpg)

<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.85rem; margin-top: -6px;"><em>Day 1 at Bristol Independent Gaming, with (nearly) everyone in the room and the nine tables numbered.</em></p>

### How it worked

Each Day 1 table winner went through to the Grand Final. Everyone else carried on in the Runners-Up games, seeded by score, initiative and home system control: six games of 6 players, plus a final 5-player game. Two of the nine Day 1 winners couldn't make it to the Grand Final, so it was played with 7 players.

Every Day 1 table winner took home an Infantry trophy. On Day 2, each game was played for a ship trophy: the War Sun for the Grand Final, then the Flagship, Dreadnought, Carrier, Cruiser, Destroyer, Fighter II and Fighter I for the Runners-Up games.

For more game statistics and in-depth analytics, see the [full tournament on Akamar](https://akamar.app/tournaments/leders-of-the-imperium-2026-10).
