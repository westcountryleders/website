Day 1 of our October 2026 Twilight Imperium tournament, *Leders of the Imperium*, took place on Saturday 10 October.

On Table 1, six players fought it out across the galaxy. Nik took the win with 10 points, the only player to reach the full 10, and did it despite losing a home planet. Kirk finished one point behind on 9. Josh, Filip and Chris all ended on 8 points, with the tiebreak decided by initiative order, and Maciek completed the table on 7.

| Place | Player | Points | Home planets | Initiative |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | Nik (winner) | 10 | Lost one | 6 |
| 2 | Kirk | 9 | All held | 8 |
| 3 | Josh | 8 | All held | 0 |
| 4 | Filip | 8 | All held | 3 |
| 5 | Chris | 8 | All held | 5 |
| 6 | Maciek | 7 | All held | 1 |
