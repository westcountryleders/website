Table 4 went the distance, reaching Round 6, which is typically a round or two longer than expected, and the scores made for a close end game. Rory took the win with 10 points and every home planet intact. Conrad, Adam and Ashley all finished on 8: Conrad took second with all his home planets, while Adam and Ashley had each lost one, with Adam ahead on initiative. Tristan and Will finished on 7. Everyone was still on cordial terms after the game.

Rory sadly couldn't play on Day 2, so missed out on the Grand Final.
