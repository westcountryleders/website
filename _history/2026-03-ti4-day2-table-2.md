Alex took the Flagship with the Nomad on 10 points, one ahead of David’s Clan of Saar on 9. Mark and Gavin tied on 8, with Mark ahead on initiative, then Neil finished on 7 and Will on 6.

The game was played on Mark’s blinged-out set, which got a special thank you from the table. Will reckoned Gavin’s Barony didn’t get enough credit for reaching 8 points with a tough faction, and wanted to know how much Gavin would have paid to stop the Nomad blowing up his PDS!
