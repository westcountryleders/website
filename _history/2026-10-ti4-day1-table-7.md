Table 7 ran to Round 5, and Adam took the win with a full 10 points, despite losing a home planet along the way. Will finished one point behind on 9. The table also had two tournament winners at it: Gavin, our October 2025 champion, and Luke, who won the Leeds TI4 Tournament in September 2026, where they played Twilight's Fall. The pair tied on 8, with Gavin taking third on initiative. James finished on 7 and Neil completed the table on 6.
