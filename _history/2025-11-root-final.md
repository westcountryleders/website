The final was a surprisingly short 5-player fight on the Mountain map, with the Tower replaced by the Lost City. Finalist Rich (⛏️ Underground Duchy) remembers the fight for woodland supremacy like this:

> For the first few rounds there was little direct conflict in the woodland. The 🦝 Vagabond adventured and aided the 🐦‍⬛ Crows while they plotted (including some good extortion of the Moles). Meanwhile the 🐦 Birds and ⛏️ Moles made an alliance to carve up the board and slowly spread out. The poor 🐁 Alliance took knocks from multiple sides and got hemmed in by the Crows’ tactically placed snares!
>
> As the game progressed, the Moles and the Woodland Alliance fell behind the pack as they struggled to get their engines online through lack of cards. The game turned more violent as the Moles, Birds and Crows chipped away at the Vagabond’s items, the Crows and Moles conspired to turmoil the Eyrie, and the Birds and Moles removed the Crows’ plots. This, however, all fed the Crows’ Coffin Makers…
>
> As the game entered the endgame, the Woodland Alliance struggled to spread due to martial law, and the Eyrie’s turmoil made them easy pickings for the nimble Vagabond and Crows. The Vagabond made a good effort but couldn’t get over the line. The Moles were set to win on their next turn, but the Crows made a daring play to attack the Mole base, which gave them just enough points to win!
