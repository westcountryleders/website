Our very first tournament was a casual Root tournament on the May Day bank holiday, Monday 1 May 2023, held at the Hen & Chicken as part of Craig’s North Street Games Day. It all started in the Discord channel a fortnight earlier, with a hope that we could find at least 8 players to make it work, and plenty of lively debate about the best format: points for wins (and half or a quarter of a point for coalition wins), a knock-out with a losers’ bracket, or round-robin games feeding into a final.

![The North Street Games poster for the Root Casual Tournament at the Hen & Chicken, Monday 1 May, 1pm](/assets/img/history/root-2023-05-poster.jpg)

On the day, a handful of us brought along every copy of Root and its expansions we owned (with print-and-play sets as back-up), and by half past two there were three games on the go. Games were played across several maps, with landmarks left up to each table and hirelings saved for any three-player games.

The final kicked off at around 8pm with the Lizard Cult, the Lord of the Hundreds, the Marquise de Cat and the Eyrie Dynasties on a very crowded board. As James predicted, whoever crafted Coffin Makers would win, and so it proved: congratulations to **Andreas**, our first ever tournament winner, who led the Lizard Cult to victory!

A big thank you to everyone who came along and brought their copies of Root, and to Craig Andrews for putting on the North Street Games Day, and to Craig, Charles, Nick and everyone else who shared their photos.
