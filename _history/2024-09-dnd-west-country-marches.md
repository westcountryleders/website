Since September 2024, West Country Leders has been running **The West Country Marches**, a drop-in Dungeons & Dragons campaign. There is no fixed party: each session, whoever can make it picks a quest from the board in the city of Lilyhaven, ventures out into the unmapped land of Umber, and brings back whatever they find for the next group to follow up.

Louis created the world of Umber and the campaign’s rules, and he and Katie have taken turns as Dungeon Master. Between adventures, characters spend downtime in the city: working, researching in the Frontier Athenæum, praying at the multi-faith meditation room, or losing their gold at the Gilded Lily. Whoever writes up the session log earns an extra downtime action, and those logs are where this history comes from.

In December 2024 the players clubbed together to give Katie and Louis the new Dungeon Master’s Guide as a thank you, signed by all of their characters.

The adventure so far, a year at a time:

### 2024: Beyond the walls

The first adventurers found the Marches sick: grass that bled, boars that rotted while still alive, and a temple of the nature goddess Martel weeping blood. Brother Bob’s party took a pig called Bacon into the Dark Forest to tempt out the goblins with a barbecue, and ended up negotiating with Mor’gash, the bugbear chieftain of the Bloodclaw Tribe. Another party dug out the Wind Spire and sang its guardian spirit free.

A missing butcher led to a cult of the two-headed phoenix, whose lair in the Bloodsoaked Cliffs held an obsidian mirror, a cursed pool and a corpse with a warning about “the entrance” under Lake Polethia. Adventurers rescued a prophetic book and a meteor from a ruined observatory, met Longtooth the kobold batrider, and teleported into the Astral Foundry, an ancient works where an archdevil had once been bound.

In December the Temple of Martel’s haunted depths were finally cleared, at the cost of two loyal mastiffs, Trevor and Spike, who are remembered with a tombstone by the door. The party met Flametooth, the kobold lord who claims to speak for the dragon god Vivax, and the year ended with the Wintertide Festival and a hunt for a pair of blizzard-making spirit wolves.

### 2025: Emberdeep and the Feywild

The new year began with the Temple of Martel reconsecrated: the party pulled a goddess statue out of an enchanted pond and gave it four “seeds” (a feather, an acorn, a flower crown and a very brave squirrel). Explorers then found **Emberdeep**, a lost dwarven trading city sunk beneath a mountain lake and guarded by shark-men.

The goblin fort was found burned. In the Dark Forest, adventurers fought the druid of a walled fey garden and laid to rest the keeper of the Moon Chapel. With the kobold batriders now firebombing Lilyhaven, the queen sent four heroes to end Flametooth. They slipped into his throne room through a chalk portal and won the fight, though only after Tyr struck a bargain with a hag that still hangs over him.

Summer brought a carnival that traps souls in mirrors, a fey knight called Rhineheart freed from the ice, a tree of vengeance in the swamp of Malice, and a trial before the fey Court of Thorns, settled by a boat race. In the autumn the party went back to Emberdeep and found its dwarves enslaved by duergar. Working with a secret resistance, they fought their way into the barracks over several sessions.

### 2026: Debts come due

In January the duergar boss “Daddy” fell to Kusco’s fists, Emberdeep’s dwarves were freed, and the fight cost the life of their resistance leader, Darrien. The party found a unicorn guarding the tomb of a celestial storyteller, solved a poisoning at a fey masquerade ball, and paid the hag’s price, though Kusco now serves her under contract.

Since then the adventurers have rescued Emberdeep’s children from the duergar matriarch Big Mama, explored an abandoned laboratory of the ancient Xaranthian Empire, and helped the dwarven army storm the upper city. In October 2026 they finally reached the Overlord’s throne room… and the story is still being written.
