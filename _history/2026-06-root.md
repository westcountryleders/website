On Saturday 13 June 2026, our seventh Root tournament took place at Excelsior Games on Merchant Street, our first with the new **Homeland** expansion! Three games of Root were played over the day, with a few people squeezing in a fourth “fast and loose” game between Round 2 and the finals, and for several players it was their very first Root tournament.

### How it worked

We used the latest HOOT Standard Ruleset with a few modifications:

- All content was included (including Homeland), except Hirelings and Clockwork, with games intended for 4 players.
- Each table rolled for one of six maps (Autumn, Winter, Mountain, Lake, Gorge or Marsh), and chose between the Exiles and Partisans and the Squires and Disciples decks.
- Tables could add 0 to 2 landmarks, and choose whether to include one 🦝 Vagabond or the 🦨 Knaves of the Deepwood in the draft.
- The 🐦‍⬛ Crows had three plots of each type, and coalitions were banned.
- We no longer used Despot Infamy for the Vagabond, partly because of its terrible win rate in our tournaments, and partly because the wider Root community no longer uses it.

After Round 1, players went into the winners’ or losers’ bracket for Round 2, before a Final and a Runners-Up Final on the Mountain map.

### The champions

Congratulations to **Katie**, our new Champion, who took the Final with the 🦦 Riverfolk Company! Congratulations too to **Hol**, who won the Runners-Up Final.

### The stats

The new Homeland factions made quite an entrance, with the 🐸 Lilypad Diaspora the most played faction of the day (11 games) and winning 3 of them. The 🐈 Marquise de Cat had the best win rate, winning 4 of their 7 games, and the 🐀 Lord of the Hundreds won 4 of 8.

A big thank you to everyone who came, to everyone who brought their copies of Root, to Excelsior Games for hosting us, and to all of our organisers for running the day. Several first-timers said it was a phenomenal atmosphere, and we hope to see them back for more!
