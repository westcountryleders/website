On Saturday 8 November 2025, 26 Root fanatics journeyed to Excelsior Games in Bristol for our 6th Root tournament. We had players travel in from as far as the Netherlands, and one player whose first ever in-person game of Root was at this tournament (they went on to make the final!). As always, our contestants were friendly, competitive and brilliant Root players.

![The Root Tournament poster by Tin (The Foil): Excelsior! Games, November 8th](/assets/img/history/root-2025-11-poster.jpg)

### How it worked

Everyone played at least two games. If you did well in your first game, you went through to the winners’ bracket; otherwise you went into the losers’ bracket for a second game, just for fun. The three best players from the winners’ bracket went through to the final.

To spice things up, we made the 4th seat at the final table up for grabs for **one** player from the losers’ bracket: anyone who won a losers’ bracket game by dominance would go straight through, no questions asked. What we didn’t anticipate was the tenacity of your average Root player. Every. Single. Losers’ bracket game. Had a dominance play, and three of them succeeded! For a moment we were looking at a 6-player final, until one of our winners’ bracket players couldn’t stay for the final, taking it back down to five. Long story short: give board game players a loophole and they will exploit it 9 times out of 10!

### The champion

Congratulations to **Alex**, our Grand Champion, who miraculously won a 5-player final with the 🐦‍⬛ Corvid Conspiracy, with the 🦝 Tinker and the ⛏️ Moles in play too!

### Raising money for Bristol Mind

WCL also sold raffle tickets on the day for the mental health charity Bristol Mind, raising £145 and bringing the total we raised across the autumn to over £600.

### The stats

Nick once again tallied up the post-game statistics. The 🦦 Riverfolk Company had the best win rate and the highest average points, while the 🦎 Lizard Cult were the most played and most successful faction, with 3 wins from 10 games.

A huge thank you to everyone who came along and created such an enjoyable atmosphere, to everyone who brought along their copies of Root, to Excelsior Games for hosting us, and to Nick, James, Katie and Dan for working away behind the scenes.
