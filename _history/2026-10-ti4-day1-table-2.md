Kayure took Table 2 with 10 points while holding all their home planets, the only player at the table to reach the full 10. Horst and Rafal tied on 8 points, with Horst taking second on initiative. Mark and Michal tied on 7, and the same tiebreak put Mark ahead. Brendan completed the table on 6.

| Place | Player | Points | Home planets | Initiative |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | Kayure (winner) | 10 | All held | 8 |
| 2 | Horst | 8 | All held | 2 |
| 3 | Rafal | 8 | All held | 6 |
| 4 | Mark | 7 | All held | 1 |
| 5 | Michal | 7 | All held | 3 |
| 6 | Brendan | 6 | All held | 5 |
