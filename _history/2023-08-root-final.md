The final was played on the Mountain map between David M, Andreas, Fabio, Rick and Nick. Liron finished third after the first two rounds but couldn’t stay for the final, so the next player on the standings took his place.

David M’s 🐦 Eyrie Dynasties had a great opening and took a huge hitting, and the whole table was convinced it was their game. In the closing moments it looked like the Eyrie might close it out, and if not the 🐁 Woodland Alliance were looking very strong. Fabio played an excellent game, only being recognised as a threat when it was far too late: huge amounts of supporters from plenty of outrage kept the machine going, and he burst to 30 points to take the title, with the Eyrie just one point behind.
