Rafal took the Cruiser with 10 points, holding all of his home planets and the first initiative slot. Adam, our March '26 TI4 Champion, finished second on 9 points, edging out Phillip, who also had 9, on initiative order.

Luke and Mark both finished on 8 points, with Luke placed ahead on initiative, and Ashley rounded out the table on 7. Playing Council Keleres, Ashley picked Mentak's system as her home and lost Moll Primus, the only home planet lost at the table.

| Place | Player | Points | Home planets | Initiative |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | Rafal (winner) | 10 | All held | 1 |
| 2 | Adam | 9 | All held | 3 |
| 3 | Phillip | 9 | All held | 8 |
| 4 | Luke | 8 | All held | 4 |
| 5 | Mark | 8 | All held | 7 |
| 6 | Ashley | 7 | Lost one (Moll Primus) | 2 |
