Alex took the win on Table 5 with a full 10 points and every home planet intact. Behind them it was a tight race: Conrad, Edward and Truan all finished level on 9 points, with initiative order deciding second to fourth in that order. Pete finished on 8, and Sam completed the table on 6 after losing a home planet.

| Place | Player | Points | Home planets | Initiative |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | Alex (winner) | 10 | All held | 0 |
| 2 | Conrad | 9 | All held | 2 |
| 3 | Edward | 9 | All held | 3 |
| 4 | Truan | 9 | All held | 4 |
| 5 | Pete | 8 | All held | 1 |
| 6 | Sam | 6 | Lost one | 5 |
