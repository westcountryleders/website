Table 3 started off peacefully, with everyone developing, and even with area control objectives many people got to score. The Muaat let the Nekro take much of their slice, and the Nekro then purged their hero to net themselves Assault Cannon and 7 trade goods in rounds 2 and 3!

However, this peace could not last. The conniving Arborec planned a three-way support swap, then used the disjointed nature of it all to take the lands of one of their support buddies: the poor Muaat, who felt so betrayed they considered Nova Seeding them purely out of spite. Thankfully, with the co-operation of the Mentak Coalition, the Muaat were able to keep themselves in contention in the Galactic Council.

This led to an incredible final round. The Nekro Virus looked set to take the crown, with 17 infantry, their Alastor flagship and a support fleet patrolling their home world of Mordai II. They also had the Argent’s Destroyer II upgrades, so they needed to be fought with precision. With some clutch rolling, the Muaat Nova Seeding four of their destroyers, and a galaxy on edge, they were stopped just in time.

The galaxy now had to take the Nekro home world to safeguard everyone from the looming threat. The L1Z1X heeded the call, sending a plague to their home system that took out 11 of the 17 infantry before the attack had even begun. Led by the Helmsman, the L1Z1X sent the greatest fleet ever seen into battle, including their flagship [0.0.1], and decimated the remaining Nekro survivors in space, leaving Mordai II ripe for the taking. One home system wasn’t enough for the L1Z1X, though, and they soon took the Muaat home system as well!

The Muaat had reached 9 points in the round via Imperial, (barely) holding Mecatol Rex and satisfying a Stage I objective. A third contender was the Argent Flight, who with ingenious use of their hero, Mirik Aun Sissiri, spread to most of the galaxy’s empty spaces, the latest Stage II objective available. Holding the Diplomacy strategy card, they were unstoppable if the game reached the status phase.

However, with just one lonely destroyer defending Mecatol Rex while holding Imperial, and after spending all their possible actions, the Embers of Muaat were able to just about Prove Endurance and take the win! Such a close and fun end game. Big kudos to Phillip for commanding such a dominant L1Z1X, and to Luke for being such a potent threat as the Nekro Virus.
