On Saturday 19 October 2024, more than 30 forest fanatics arrived at YHA Bristol on the Harbourside from across the UK (one even made the trip from Kent!) for the fourth Root tournament hosted by West Country Leders. Some had only learned to play Root recently, while others had hundreds of games under their belt, but everyone brought a really positive and welcoming energy to the day.

![The Root Tournament poster by Tin (The Foil): YHA Bristol Harbourside, October 19th, with the Root Finalist stamp](/assets/img/history/root-2024-10-finalist-poster.jpg)

### How it worked

Every game used the Exiles and Partisans deck, Advanced Setup and randomised clearings, with Despot Infamy for the Vagabond. Your score was the total of your points across your games, with a failed dominance attempt worth 10.

- **Round 1:** nine games across the Winter, Mountain and Lake maps.
- **Round 2:** the Round 1 winners and runners-up played four winners’ bracket games, while everyone else played four losers’ bracket games.
- **The finals:** four players went through to the Winners’ Final on the Winter map, and four more played a Losers’ Bracket Final on the Autumn map.

### Final standings

Congratulations to **Terence**, our Tournament Champion, who won all three of his games: with the 🦝 Vagabond in Round 1, the 🐦‍⬛ Corvid Conspiracy in Round 2 and the 🦡 Keepers in Iron in the final, for a perfect 90 points! Terence and Annie were at their very first tournament, and Terence was the only player all day to win with the Vagabond, despite it being picked 14 times. Congratulations too to **Dino**, our Losers’ Bracket Champion with the 🦦 Riverfolk Company.

### The stats

Nick once again totalled up the statistics from every game. The 🦡 Keepers in Iron were one of the most played factions, with only the 🦝 Vagabond picked more often. The 🐦‍⬛ Corvid Conspiracy had the best win rate (2 wins from 3 games), despite being one of the least played factions of the day, and the 🦦 Riverfolk Company scored the most points on average. Compared with our previous tournament, the ⛏️ Underground Duchy didn’t win nearly as often. Perhaps we’ve all learned to hit them nice and early!
