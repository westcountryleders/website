Table 1 finished their draft in record time, weeks ahead of the event, and their game was a close one. Francesco took the win with the Nomad on 10 points. Behind him, Will, Adam and Rafal all finished on 8, separated by initiative order, while Ben and Adam (Twitch) ended on 7. With two Adams at the table, the “Council of Adams” was quickly formed.

After the game, Will declared that he expected a Table 1 win from Francesco in the Grand Final the next day.
