Our second ever Bristol Root tournament took place on the August bank holiday, Monday 28 August 2023, back at the Hen & Chicken as part of Craig’s North Street Games Day. We had a turnout of 21, with players travelling in from far and wide, including Andreas, who flew back from Berlin to defend his title!

![The West Country Leders and North Street Games poster for the Root Casual Tournament, 28 August, 1pm at the Hen & Chicken](/assets/img/history/root-2023-08-poster.jpg)

### How it worked

Everyone played two rounds, with five games running at the same time in each (four games of four and one of five). A win earned 2 tournament points and second place earned 1, with ties broken by turn order (the player who went later getting the edge). The top five went through to a 5-player final, playing for prize vouchers kindly provided by Leder Games.

All 10 factions were played at some point, with lots of interesting mixes, and the 🐁 Woodland Alliance proved the faction of the day, winning 4 of its 7 games. There was also an amazing 🐈 Marquise de Cat dominance play from Aaron, who had never played a game of Root before, that came very close to winning his second game!

### Final standings

1. **Fabio**: Champion, with an explosive 🐁 Woodland Alliance burst to 30 points in the final, after winning his second-round game with the ⛏️ Underground Duchy.
2. **David M**: just one point behind with the 🐦 Eyrie Dynasties, who looked to have the final won until a last sneaky Rat ambush.
3. **Andreas**: our returning champion, with the 🦝 Vagabond (Ronin) on 22.
4. **Nick**: with the 🦎 Lizard Cult on 19.
5. **Rick**: with the 🐀 Lord of the Hundreds on 15.

![The tournament standings after Rounds 1 and 2, with the finalists highlighted](/assets/img/history/root-2023-08-standings.png)
