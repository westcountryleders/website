The Grand Final brought together all eight Day 1 winners for an epic 8-player finale. Rufus drafted first, with the draft carrying on into the Sunday morning. After five rounds, a new Galactic Champion was crowned: congratulations to Gavin, who led the Ghosts of Creuss to victory on 10 points, having led the Argent Flight to victory the day before. Richard’s L1Z1X Mindnet and Rúadhán’s Nomad were right behind on 9, followed by Connor’s Mentak Coalition on 8.
