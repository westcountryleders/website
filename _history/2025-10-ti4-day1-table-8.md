It was all peace, love and bliss on Table 8 until the final round, when the objective revealed needed 8 ships in one system. The Barony of Letnev jumped for joy (most unlike them) when this was revealed, as their hero, Darktalon Treilla, was perfectly set up for it. The only thing stopping them was their initiative: the Yin Brotherhood held Leadership and the Barony held Diplomacy. Both could have stopped the other, but neither took any action.

So the Ghosts of Creuss had to step in. Using their advanced knowledge of wormhole technology, they moved the Yin’s Lodor system close to the Creuss homeland with Riftwalker Meian, moved the Ghost fleet next to the Darien system, and then, with Fleet Logistics, attacked the Yin in their home system, killing enough ships to stop them scoring with a fleet there. They followed up with an attack on the newly built Yin fleet in Lodor, led by the Hil Colish flagship. The battle started with the Yin being Dimensionally Spliced, and ended with the Creuss achieving total victory and winning over the Council by unveiling their flagship.

Meanwhile, the Arborec caught up in the eyes of the Council, taking Mecatol Rex with War Suns and scoring with the Imperial strategy card. The Clan of Saar were spent by this point, having fought tough battles over Mecatol Rex in earlier rounds and invaded the Arborec slice.

During all this, the Council of Keleres jumped for glee as they crept ever closer to victory. Oliver’s Barony of Letnev reached 10 points and won the game, but the leaders of the Yin Brotherhood and the Barony of Letnev were unable to continue the fight into the next day, so Rufus’s Council of Keleres, also on 10, went through to the Grand Final as the default winners of this wild game.
