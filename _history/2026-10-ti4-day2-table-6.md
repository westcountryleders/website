The Destroyer game was a long, tense six-player affair that went on for about nine hours, and it came down to a quiet win in the status phase. Michal's Argent Flight took the victory on 10 points, with Joe's Ghosts of Creuss just behind on 9 and Ben's Naalu Collective third on 8.

Michal's game did not start well. He received the Naalu alliance and Support for the Throne very early via Mindsieve, and enjoyed Joe leaving Judicial Abolishment on top of the agenda deck so that he and Naalu could not see the next card. His secret objectives were less kind: Prove Endurance, then Occupy the Seat of the Empire with Sardakk N'orr taking Mecatol Rex and massing next door, then Adapt New Strategies, which left him stuck on three techs of one colour. In round 3 he sent a big fleet into the Fracture to grab relics just before Naalu could, picking up some good combat relics and Junior. Meanwhile Naalu's push for Styx was slowed by a war with the Ghosts, which started when Naalu took Tempesta in the Ghosts' slice and ended with the Ghosts in Naalu's home system.

Round 4 saw Michal deal with Sardakk and take Mecatol Rex, turning Prove Endurance into Dictate Policy and drawing Destroy Their Greatest Ship. The Empyrean locked most of Naalu's forces in the Fracture with a Void Tether, then released them in exchange for Gift of Prescience, letting Naalu take Styx and the shard. In round 5, Michal kept his word to the Empyrean and took Leadership so they could have Imperial, while Sardakk took Construction for the stage 2 objective. The action phase brought the game's big moment. Michal had Lightwave Deflector in play and fought for Styx, but the Empyrean attacked with a huge force, then used Fleet Logistics, the Codex and Overrule to reach 8 points holding Mecatol Rex with Imperial still unplayed. The rest of the table orchestrated a response: Michal killed the Empyrean flagship with just enough force (quietly scoring an action phase secret that Joe had correctly called), Yssaril's James softened the ground, and the Ghosts came in with a large force and won the ground combat on good rolls.

With the Empyrean stopped, everyone's forces were depleted, and Michal was sitting on five structures that nobody was looking at too closely. Imperial gave him a better secret, Hoard Raw Materials, which he could score without doing anything. The Ghosts took Naalu's home system and the shard, which put them on 9 with an unscored secret, and Michal made a weak, desperate-looking attack on their home to make his own secret look unscoreable while he reinforced his PDS with mechs. The last threat was Yssaril, who had one tactic token left, but they went after undefended planets to finally score their six-planet public objective instead. Michal scored his secret in the status phase for the win. Good game, all!
