Table 3 came down to the wire, with three players finishing level on 9 points. Jo won on the tiebreak playing The Firmament, ahead of David on initiative, while Adam's Yin Brotherhood took third after losing a home planet. Fin was next on 8, with Joe and James both on 7. Joe edged fifth place on initiative.

Joe and Jo tended to follow each other in the initiative order every round, so the table quickly took to calling them Jo #1 and Joe #2.

Joe's Keleres took Mecatol Rex in Round 1 and kept the agendas coming, with plenty of action phase agendas through the game. The table was playing on Adam's copy of the game, and got to appreciate his flagships along the way.

Thunder's Edge was another talking point. Adam's Yin took it and held on to it for the entire game, despite a brave attempt by James's Clan of Saar to take it off him. Adam also very nearly handed it to James through the Colonial Redistribution agenda, with the planet sitting in an asteroid field, before the rest of the table put a stop to that. Saar hobbled up to 7 points.

The big gamble came late on. Sitting on 9 points, Adam rolled for the Yin's Silver Flame, going for the win, but it didn't pay off: he stayed on 9 and lost a home planet. [Watch the roll on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/Gag2XfaLk1c).
