Truan's Crimson Rebellion took the Flagship with 10 points, holding all of his home planets. Will's Naaz-Rokha Alliance and David's Xxcha Kingdom both finished on 9, with Will placed second on initiative order.

Edward's Empyrean finished on 8 points, while Kirk's Vuil'Raith Cabal and Conrad's Titans of Ul both ended on 6, with Kirk ahead on initiative. Every player held all of their home planets at the end of the game.
