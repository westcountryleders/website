The Grand Final brought together the seven winners from Day 1, all vying for control of the galaxy. Alex's Nomad took the War Sun with 10 points, after one of the most explosive turns of the weekend.

Nik had to rush off at 7pm, so Conrad (aka DarkAura, from Table 2) stepped in to play the Naalu Collective, but he never got a turn. Alex's Nomad, on 6 points, scored 4 in a single turn, taking three actions by combining Fleet Logistics with the Thunder's Edge ability:

1. A tiny space combat to kill one of Muaat's destroyers and score Betray a Friend (with Fleet Logistics).
2. A tactical move into the L1Z1X home system, scoring the Unveil Flagship secret objective in space and then taking control of [0.0.0] (with the Thunder's Edge ability).
3. Imperial on initiative 8, to score Conquer the Weak.

The Naalu held Coup d'État to try to stop the Imperial, but Alex's Nomad had Sabotage, which made the whole combo virtually unstoppable.
