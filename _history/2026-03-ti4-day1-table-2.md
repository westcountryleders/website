Table 2 was a truly international affair, with players travelling from Plymouth, Stoke-on-Trent and even Treviso, near Venice, to take part. Marc, who turned out to be the only Bristolian at the table, took the win with the Nekro Virus on 10 points, just one ahead of Neil’s Titans of Ul. Michael and Mike tied on 7, with Michael ahead on initiative, then Daf finished on 6 and Michele on 5.

With three Mikes and a Marc at the table, the pre-game chat soon turned into the founding of the “cult of Mike”.
