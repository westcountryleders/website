On Saturday 27 September 2025, we held our second Arcs tournament: a casual mini tournament at Excelsior Games on Merchant Street, running from 10am to 6pm.

![West Country Leders Arcs mini Tournament poster: Saturday September 27th, 10am to 6pm](/assets/img/history/arcs-2025-09-poster.jpg)

### How it worked

Twelve players signed up. Everyone was dealt one Leader and one Lore card, and played a first-round game. The four first-round winners then met in a 4-player Winners’ Final, with games for 2nd and 3rd place played alongside it.

### The champion

Congratulations to **Barnie**, our Arcs champion, who won the Winners’ Final with the Warrior on 31 points! Barnie’s trophy has since been on quite the adventure, as the photos in the Final section show. Congratulations too to **Katie**, who won the 2nd-place game.

A big thank you to everyone who came along, and to Excelsior Games for hosting us.
