The Fighter II game was a six-player table, and it came down to a tense final round in which almost everyone had a plausible route to victory. David took the win with 11 points, with Neil and Will both on 9 and Neil placed ahead on initiative. Alex finished on 8 and Brendan on 7, while Ghazi's Clan of Saar were eliminated in the closing moments.

Saar had taken an early point lead, so the rest of the table piled in: everyone took their planets, destroyed their last space dock and isolated them on Mecatol Rex. That is no small feat, as Saar are especially difficult to eliminate with their immunity in asteroid fields. The Ixthian Artifact was in play, but it failed to blow up and handed out technology instead. Keleres then tried for Mecatol Rex and lost a tragic number of ships in a gravity rift, before the Barony of Letnev sent in a fleet of around 15 ships. They won the space combat but only just failed to win the ground combat, leaving two damaged mechs alive.

Near the end of the round it was clear that Neil's Argent Flight would reach 10 points with Diplomacy before David's Mahact could get there with Warfare. David had a plan to use the Mahact hero to move the huge fleet out of Argent's home system, but it was risky. Then Alex spotted that Saar and Argent had swapped Support for the Throne, so eliminating Saar would also stop Argent reaching 10. David used the hero to clear out the Barony's massive fleet, finished off the Clan of Saar, and took the win. Good game, all!
