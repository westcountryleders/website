### Winners’ Final

Terence (🦡 Keepers in Iron), Leo (🦝 Vagabond), Joe (🦎 Lizard Cult) and Alex (🐦 Eyrie Dynasties) met in the Winners’ Final on the Winter map. It was a very competitive game, and Terence reached 30 to complete his perfect day and become our Tournament Champion.

| Turn | Player | Faction | Score |

| 1 | Joe | 🦎 Lizard Cult | 27 |

| 4 | Alex | 🐦 Eyrie Dynasties | 24 |
