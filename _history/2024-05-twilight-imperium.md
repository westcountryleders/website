Our May 2024 Twilight Imperium tournament took place in Bristol over the late May bank holiday weekend, with 36 players across 6 tables of 6. Day 1 was played at Excelsior Games on Sunday 26 May, and Day 2 at Replay Bristol on Monday 27 May. With negotiations starting before the tournament did (there was plenty of debate over whether the Milty Draft should have 9 or 7 factions alongside the 7 slices to pick), it was clear we were in for plenty of diplomacy!

### How it worked

Sunday was made up of 6 games of 6 players, each with its own Milty Draft. The winner of each table took home a gold medallion and went through to the Final on the Monday, played for the Gold War Sun. The players who came 2nd went on to the Silver Starlights game, played for the Silver Flagship, and those who came 3rd went on to the Bronze Buddies game, played for the Bronze Dreadnought. Where someone couldn’t make the Monday, the next player from their table took their place.

### Final standings

1. **Duncan**: Tourney Champion, winning the Final with the Federation of Sol after leading a fleet-less Sardakk N’orr to victory on Table 4.
2. **Maciek**: winning the Silver Starlights game and the Silver Flagship with the Universities of Jol-Nar, after coming 2nd on Table 5 with the Vuil’Raith Cabal.
3. **Rich B**: winning the Bronze Buddies game and the Bronze Dreadnought with the Naaz-Rokha Alliance, after playing the Xxcha Kingdom on Table 3.
