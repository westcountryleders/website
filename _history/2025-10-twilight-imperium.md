Our October 2025 Twilight Imperium tournament, *Leders of the Imperium*, took place at Bristol Independent Gaming over the weekend of 18 and 19 October, with 48 players across 8 tables of 6 on Day 1, and 37 dedicated souls returning for Day 2.

![The Leders of the Imperium 2025 tournament sheet, with Bristol-themed units, the schedule and the rules](/assets/img/history/ti4-2025-10-poster.jpg)

<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 0.85rem; margin-top: -6px;"><em>The tournament sheet, complete with the SS Great Britain flagship, HMS Bristol destroyer and the Bristol Beaufighter.</em></p>

### How it worked

Saturday was made up of 8 games of 6 players, with randomised seating and the same six slices at every table, but randomised factions. Nevertheless, it was a Battle of the Brothers, as the Yin Brotherhood appeared in 5 of the 8 games! Our reigning champion Duncan, who had piloted Sardakk N’orr and the Federation of Sol to the Galactic Throne in previous campaigns, defended his title as the Nekro Virus.

Each Day 1 table winner went through to an 8-player Grand Final on the Sunday, played for the War Sun trophy. Everyone else was seated into Runners-Up games by their Day 1 points, then initiative in the final round, then their faction’s win rate. The winners of the 2nd, 3rd, 4th and 5th place games took those places overall, and a sixth game was played with the Discordant Stars expansion.

### Final standings

1. **Gavin**: Galactic Emperor, winning the Grand Final with the Ghosts of Creuss after taking Table 3 with the Argent Flight on Saturday.
2. **Peter**: Galactic Vice Chancellor, winning the 2nd place game with the Empyrean after coming extremely close with the Naalu Collective on Saturday.
3. **James**: Deputy Vice Chancellor, winning the 3rd place game with the Embers of Muaat in a very clutch final round, after a middling game as the Ghosts of Creuss on Saturday (spent kingslaying poor Liam’s Yin Brotherhood).
4. **Lawrence**, who also clinched it with the Embers of Muaat, after playing as the Clan of Saar on Saturday.
5. **Conrad**, winning as the Mentak Coalition and generously sharing his victory with Eli of the Emirates of Hacan, who not only covered a last-minute dropout but was playing Twilight Imperium at just 12 years of age. Kudos!

The Discordant Stars game was won by Chris H, who piloted the Florzen Profiteers to victory after playing the Clan of Saar the day before.
