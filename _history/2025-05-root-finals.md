### Winners’ Final

JT (🦦 Riverfolk Company), Hunter (🦡 Keepers in Iron), Annie (🐦 Eyrie Dynasties) and Sam (🐦‍⬛ Corvid Conspiracy) played a very close Winners’ Final on the Autumn map. Annie’s Eyrie went for a bunny dominance, Sam’s Crows snuck up the scoreboard in the most Corvid-like way, and JT got it over the line with some crazy card pulls at the very end.

|:--|:--|:--|:--|

| 2 | Hunter | 🦡 Keepers in Iron | 18 |
| 3 | Annie | 🐦 Eyrie Dynasties | Dominance (failed) |
| 4 | Sam | 🐦‍⬛ Corvid Conspiracy | 26 |
