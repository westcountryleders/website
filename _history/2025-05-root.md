On Saturday 10 May 2025, West Country Leders hosted our 5th Root tournament. 34 Root fanatics met up on the sunny Harbourside in Bristol for an all-day competition to see who could claim the title of Forest Champion! We were joined by Root players from all across the South of England, and a couple of our friends from Canada even crafted Boat Builders to make the trip across the Atlantic.

![The Root Tournament poster by Tin (The Foil): YHA Bristol Harbourside, May 10th, doors at 10am](/assets/img/history/root-2025-05-poster.jpg)

There was a lot of really competitive play from everyone, and a fantastic energy from players who won and lost their games with good grace and excellent sportsmanship.

### How it worked

Everyone played a first-round game, then moved into a winners’ or losers’ bracket for Round 2. The four Round 2 winners’ bracket winners met in the Winners’ Final on the Autumn map, while a Losers’ Bracket Final was played on the Lake map.

### The champions

Congratulations to **JT**, our new Champion, who won the Winners’ Final with the 🦦 Riverfolk Company! It was an extremely tense game, with plenty of table talk: the 🐦 Eyrie went for a bunny dominance and, had JT not pulled off the win with some crazy card draws at the death, the Eyrie may well have won it that way. Congratulations too to **Zac**, our Losers’ Bracket Champion, also with the 🦦 Riverfolk Company. The Otters won both finals!

### Prizes

Leder Games kindly provided $200 of Leder store vouchers, split between the finalists. Our brilliant team also put together a collection of smaller prizes: Jarred grew plants for the finalists, Katie printed custom faction board name tags, Nick printed Root meeples for everyone who won a game, and Tin brought custom t-shirts (as well as designing our poster).

### The stats

The 🐀 Lord of the Hundreds did really well this time, with the 🦦 Riverfolk Company a close second on overall win rate. There were no ⛏️ Moles in either final, which Liam (who had lost to the Moles twice that week) was very happy about!

![Contestant highlights: a tantalisingly close end game, five of the Eyrie’s roosts taken out in one turn, Lizards forming an unbreakable wall to protect the Moles, and more](/assets/img/history/root-2025-05-highlights.jpg)

A big thank you to everyone who came along, particularly those who brought their own copies of Root for us to use, and to the organisers who put in so much work beforehand and on the day. You can also see the original [Sway presentation](https://sway.cloud.microsoft/kF6Diacd5kRWo7Go).
