The six Day 1 winners converged on one galaxy for complete control of the universe. Duncan is a human Twilight Imperium encyclopaedia, and it showed: he picked Politics twice (making his neighbour, Rich’s Naalu Collective, speaker for free), then took it himself in the penultimate round so he could take Leadership and beat David’s Xxcha Kingdom, sitting on 9 points with the Peace Turtles holding Imperial, to become the Supreme Galactic Emperor.

Katie’s Mahact Gene-Sorcerers tried to interfere, sending the Xxcha’s second-biggest fleet on a suicide run into the Naalu Grand Armada, before James’s Argent Flight came in to take Quann and remove three of the space cannons in David’s vast array.

However, nobody could stop Duncan’s Federation of Sol as they traversed the gamma wormholes to defeat Ben’s Arborec in a space battle in Everra’s nebula, then took two of the Naalu’s industrial planets to score *Corner the Market* and *Monopolize Production*, clinching him the well-deserved title.
